# Provenance and distribution notes

## Newly constructed parts

The SVG assets, vector construction script, tokens, gallery and implementation documents were created for this Crown & Frontier request. They do not embed third-party icon collections or extracted game artwork. They may be adapted as project assets. This pack is not an assertion of exclusive copyright or a new license for the existing game repository.

## Generated landmark artwork

`landmarks/*.png` are AI-generated illustrations created with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-06, using the user's approved mockup for visual direction. They are not hand-drawn artwork. Exact prompts are in `sources/landmark-prompts.json`. Raster originals are preserved without postprocessing. Similarity and rendering consistency should be evaluated in the running game; these four illustrations do not imply exclusive visual ownership or historical accuracy.

## Fonts

Barlow Condensed: Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed

Source Sans 3: Adobe; copyright and Reserved Font Name notices are in the supplied license. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sourcesans3

Font binaries were downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository on 2026-10-06. Complete upstream TTF files and OFL 1.1 notices are retained. WOFF2 files are lossless format conversions of those binaries, without subsetting or renaming. Keep the corresponding OFL files with redistributed fonts. Font licenses do not require placing your game code under the OFL.

## Map geography and faction identities

No third-party geographic dataset, real-world map image, official coat of arms, soundtrack or licensed game asset is bundled. Flags are generic fictional designs named for their geometric charge; verify their fit before assigning them to existing factions. The requested Earth/Europe geography must be obtained and attributed separately during implementation.
